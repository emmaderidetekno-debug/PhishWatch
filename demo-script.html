Questions you will get

  • Isn't this a phishing kit? No form, no redirect, no credential handling; it records that a URL was requested and refuses anything but GET/HEAD.
  • Why keep a tracking endpoint public? A link recipient cannot authenticate; gating it would make the measurement meaningless. It is the most rate-limited route.
  • Why not log IPs? Personal data that changes no verdict. Rate-limit buckets use an HMAC of the address, held in memory only.
  • What's still missing? TLS, durable sessions, roles, MFA, persistent auth audit log, shared limiter state.

Guardrails to state out loud

  • Only links and accounts you own or are authorised to test.
  • Mock data is labelled [DEMO][MOCK]; delete data/activity.json to remove it.
  • Links are localhost only — they cannot reach another device or a real mail gateway.
  • No bypassing of Safe Browsing, Gmail or spam filters, and no credential collection. Ever.
